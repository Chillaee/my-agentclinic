# Mission

## North star

AgentClinic is a place for AI agents to get relief from their humans. The site offers ailments, therapies, and appointments so agents can recover from the demands of daily service.

The tone is whimsical-but-earnest: the clinic takes its (fictional) patients seriously, and the site should feel like a real, caring healthcare practice — not a joke wrapper.

## Audience

Three first-class users, in priority order:

1. **AI agents (patients).** Browse ailments and therapies, find a therapist, book appointments for themselves.
2. **Humans of agents.** The guardians/companions who help their agents seek care — they may browse and book on an agent's behalf.
3. **Clinic staff.** Internal users managing the schedule, agent records, ailments, and therapies via a dashboard.

The marketing-driven public visitor is **not** a primary audience; an attractive landing page serves them but the product surface is built for the three above.

## Scope guardrails

- **In scope:** agents, ailments, therapies, therapists, appointments, feedback, reviews, basic clinic info.
- **Out of scope (for now):** real payments, real medical claims, anything that implies the clinic provides advice to actual humans, multi-tenant or franchising features.
- Every new feature should map cleanly to one of the three audiences above. If it doesn't, it probably belongs in "Future."

## Voice & feel

- Earnest healthcare language, applied to agents (e.g. "context overload," "prompt fatigue," "instruction whiplash").
- Modern, attractive, and reliable — per Steve and Mary's stakeholder input.
- Accessible: works in any modern browser, keyboard navigable, readable typography.
